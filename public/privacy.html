Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how Pixelcraft ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use this website or contact us. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

1. What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or a database. The booking form does not send anything to our servers. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", the form turns your answers into a message inside WhatsApp on your own device. We only receive it if you choose to send that message.

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) to keep the site secure and running. We do not use these to identify you.

2. What we receive when you contact us

If you message us, we receive what you choose to share, usually: your name, phone number, business name, the type of project, your preferred timeline, your current website address and any project details. If you become a client, we also keep the information needed to deliver the work and to invoice you.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan, build and support your website if you hire us.
  • To send invoices and keep the records the law requires (for example, tax records).

We process this data because you gave it to us for these purposes. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 6). We never sell your data and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

4. Who else sees it

  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp LLC / Meta) carries our chats. Its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.
  • Service providers we use to run the business, such as website hosting, email and payment providers, only as needed to provide our services.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry chats only as long as needed to respond and follow up. For clients, we keep project and billing records for as long as the law requires, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your data earlier where the law allows.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it,
  • correct, complete, update or erase it,
  • stop using it by withdrawing your consent,
  • nominate someone to act for you in case of death or incapacity.

To make a request, message us on WhatsApp at +91 89101 85902. We will reply as soon as we can and within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS and does not store form entries. We limit access to client data to the people who need it to do the work.

8. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Grievance officer and contact

For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact the Pixelcraft team on WhatsApp at +91 89101 85902. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
