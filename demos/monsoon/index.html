How we brew

Four minutes, no shortcuts

  1. 01

    Grind

    Medium-fine, seconds before brewing. Never the night before.

  2. 02

    Bloom

    A splash of water at 93°C. The grounds puff up and breathe out.

  3. 03

    Pour

    Slow spirals, then steamed milk folded in from the edge.

  4. 04

    Sip

    Both hands round the cup. Window seat if you're lucky.
